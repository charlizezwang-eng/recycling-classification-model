Recycling Classification Model

Project Description

Every year, more than 30% of recyclable and compostable trash is improperly managed, with much of it ending up in landfills or being improperly disposed of. More often than not, this is because of people don’t know whether a piece of trash is recyclable, compostable, or landfill. When they aren’t sure how to dispose of their trash, most people just assume it’s landfill, even if it’s recyclable or compostable. The Recycling Classification Model aims to eliminate this problem by providing an easy way for people to identify the correct disposal method for their trash.

Impact

Due to our environmental state, proper waste and recycling management has never been more important. Educating people on how to manage their household waste is more important than ever. This model could help people correctly separate their waste, which would boost the amount of sustainable energy created.

Algorithm

To create my model, I first uploaded a dataset from Kaggle. I then split the data into three folders: train, test, and val, so the model could train effectively. 

After the model finished training I created a python file that used a live camera feed that ran for ten frames and classified what the object was out of thirty different types of common trash. I then created a function that printed the correct disposal method for that piece of trash.
